# Decision Optimization and AI Systems

## Overview
Decision Optimization combines mathematical programming, Operations Research (OR), and modern Artificial Intelligence to automate complex business choices under constraints.

## Integration with Agentic RAG
Modern enterprise architectures integrate RAG agents with Decision Optimization engines. The RAG agent extracts constraints, objective function parameters, and business rules from unstructured policy documents (PDFs, contracts, guidelines) and structures them into linear programming or mixed-integer programming (MIP) formulations.

Key benefits:
- **Constraint Parsing**: Automated translation of unstructured contracts into mathematical bounds.
- **Explainable Analytics**: Natural language explanations for solver decision outputs.
- **Adaptive Execution**: Real-time feedback loops when constraint violations occur.
